### 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm an **MLOps Engineer at GCash**: productionizing ML models, building a **centralized observability platform** for ML and data pipelines across the company, deploying data applications on **Kubernetes**, and driving **root cause analysis** to keep systems and models reliable.

Before GCash, I worked as a **Software Engineer** at **Senti AI**, **Demand Science**, and **Kumu**. That software engineering background shapes how I approach ML: as a **system design problem**, not just a modeling one.

My work rests on four pillars: **data pipeline engineering**, **observability**, **system architecture**, and **operational governance**. In every case I want to cut operational noise, find root causes fast, and give teams the visibility they need to deploy and scale with confidence.

### 🚀 Featured Work

#### 🧬 Strand: Enterprise Data Lineage Platform
Designed and built a lineage platform that maps **table-level and column-level dependencies** across upstream and downstream systems.
- Adopted as the **department standard** for incident triage and data quality investigation
- Lets engineers **assess blast radius** and isolate pipeline anomalies *before* they reach production models or decision systems

#### 💥 Blast Analysis Dashboard
Built a monitoring application that **clusters tables by downstream blast radius** and correlates them with rising incident frequency.
- Moved engineering effort from reactive firefighting to **targeted, high-leverage refactoring**
- **Reduced recurring production outages**

#### 📊 Feature Mart Visualizer
Designed an automated observability tool for **daily feature mart health**.
- Replaced **manual daily inspections** with an automated alerting workflow
- Delivers alerts through **chat, email, and reporting dashboards**

#### 📘 MLOps Operating Model & SOPs
Led the creation of the **standard operating procedures, documentation, and delegation frameworks** for the MLOps function.
- Set clear ways of working across **ML engineering, research, and data operations**
- Removed cross-department bottlenecks and **clarified ownership of shared infrastructure**
